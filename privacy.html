FixPro by Global Nova Technologies

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

FixPro ("we", "us") is a home-services app made by Global Nova Technologies. It connects customers with independent service providers such as electricians, plumbers and AC technicians. This policy explains what information the FixPro app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: name, phone number, email address and city.
  • Profile photo, if you choose to add one.
  • Saved addresses you add for bookings.
  • Messages you send in chat, including photos, shared locations and voice messages.
  • Ratings and reviews you write about a completed job.
  • Reports you submit about a user or a job.

Additional information from service providers

  • Services offered, experience, working hours and service area.
  • Portfolio photos of past work.
  • CNIC number and photos of the front and back of the CNIC, used only to verify identity.

Information collected when you use the app

  • Precise location, only while you use the app: for customers to find nearby providers and set the job address; for providers to receive nearby jobs and show their arrival while on a job.
  • Microphone audio, only while you are recording a voice message or on a call. FixPro never records in the background.
  • Booking activity: requests, visit charges, job status and job history.

FixPro does not use advertising, advertising IDs or third-party analytics, and does not sell your personal information.

2. How we use information

  • To create and run your account.
  • To match customers with nearby providers and manage bookings.
  • To let customers and providers chat and call each other about a job.
  • To show ratings and reviews that help others choose a provider.
  • To verify providers and keep the community safe, including reviewing reports.
  • To provide support and to comply with the law.

3. When information is shared

  • Between the customer and the provider of a booking: name, profile photo, rating, chat messages, and the job location. A customer's exact address is shared with a provider only after the customer confirms the booking.
  • Public reviews: your rating, review text and first name may be shown on the provider's profile.
  • Service providers to Global Nova Technologies that run parts of FixPro for us, under confidentiality obligations:
    • Google Firebase: account sign-in, the database, and delivering notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging uses a device token for this).
    • Cloudinary: storing photos and voice messages sent in chat, and profile photos.
    • ZEGOCLOUD: carrying the audio of in-app voice calls between the customer and the provider. Calls are not recorded.
    • Veevo Tech (SMS provider in Pakistan): sending your one-time login code to your phone number.
    • Cloudflare: a small secure server that sends notifications and login codes.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law or to protect the safety of users.

Maps are loaded from Esri (ArcGIS) map servers, which receive the area of the map being viewed and your IP address, as with any website. Map data © Esri and OpenStreetMap contributors.

4. Security

Data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to personal data is limited to people who need it to run the service. CNIC images are used only for verification.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, saved addresses, messages, reviews and provider documents are deleted within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (for example, records of payments or safety reports), which are kept only as long as required.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and update: edit your profile at any time in the app.
  • Permissions: you can turn off location or microphone access in your phone settings. Some features will not work without them.
  • Delete your account: in the app go to Profile → Delete account, or follow the steps on our account deletion page.

7. Children

FixPro is not intended for children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
